usage of LLMs

previously, I help very strong opinions against the usage of AI in software development, especially free/colaborative software, mostly due to low code quality and doubts on licensing (due to proprietary models feeding on free software)

currently, I recognize the capacity of newer models to assist in coding; however I still don't utilize AI assistance in any of my software licensed under GPLv3, as I enjoy software development and fear it negatively affects the social aspects of it
